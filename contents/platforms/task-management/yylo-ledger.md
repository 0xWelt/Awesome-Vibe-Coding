YYLO Ledger is a Git-native task and Record store with a shell-friendly CLI for developers, automation authors, and coding-agent workflows that need reviewable current state, append-only history, dependency-aware work, and bounded queries without making a database the source of truth. Ledger owns Records and task history, spanning wiki, workflow, and artifact Records alongside tasks with dependencies. Coding-agent and task/merge/release orchestration lives in YYLO CLI, and evaluation runs and their private evidence registry in YYLO Benchmark.
