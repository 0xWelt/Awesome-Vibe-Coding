Patchy Cloud is a private cloud for a company's internal tools, in beta. Anyone on the team asks their AI agent (Claude Code, Codex or another coding agent) for a tool; the agent builds it and publishes it in one step with `patchy publish`, behind the company login. Every publish is kept as a version with its author and can be rolled back, and only a tool's owner can publish to it. Tools keep their own tables and files and read company data an admin has connected, such as a Postgres database, without the tool ever holding the credentials. The beta is free, for US companies using a Chromium browser on a computer, and companies join through a waitlist.
