Voice AI agent for Mac (macOS 15+, Apple silicon), with a Windows beta. Press Fn+Space in any app and say the task; speech is recognized on the device (English and Chinese). It hands coding tasks to the Claude Code, Codex, DeepSeek Harness or opencode CLIs on your machine, runs them in the background and asks you when a step needs approval, by voice or on your phone. An Agents page lists every session, with a git worktree per task and one-click PRs. Closed source; free to download, with free daily uses of the built-in model after sign-in, optional one-time passes, or your own API key.
