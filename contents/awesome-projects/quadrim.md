Four in a row in a 4×4×4 cube, a Flutter game for iOS and Android vibe coded with Claude Code. The repository publishes the whole process next to the code: every prompt in order, four rewrites of the idea, the spec, 48 tickets and a log of 396 decisions, each with the question it answered and what it cost. Includes 31 computer opponents, the strongest playing from Oren Patashnik's 1977 solution of the game.
