Junie is JetBrains' AI coding agent. Junie Local runs inside JetBrains IDEs and Junie CLI runs in the terminal. It is built on the IntelliJ IDEA engine, supports 10+ models with bring-your-own-key, and lets you review, guide, and approve changes as it works. Free to start, with no credit card or subscription required.
