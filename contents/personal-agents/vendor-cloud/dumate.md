DuMate (百度搭子) is Baidu's assistant that carries out a request across apps and files, instead of stopping at an answer.

**Runtime:** An app you install. The [product page](https://www.dumate.cn/) says it runs on the device, with folder-level permissions, and that data stays on the device. A second confirmation is required for risky operations.

**Acts on:** Files and other applications. The product page describes sorting a folder, converting among Word, Excel, PPT, and PDF, pulling structured notes out of contracts and reports, and moving data between applications. Scheduled tasks run at a time or condition you set. Recorded steps can be replayed.

**Extensions:** Built-in skills, skills it can create, and connectors. The same page lists expert kits and an enterprise edition aimed at connecting knowledge, systems, and workflows.

**Notes:** The product page publishes a free personal plan and paid Pro and Max plans. Use that page for the current prices. This entry is DuMate, the named assistant, not a general Baidu chat product.
