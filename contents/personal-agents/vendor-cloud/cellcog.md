CellCog hires an AI employee for a role. The employee has a name, an inbox, a schedule of shifts, and a computer, and anything risky waits for your yes.

**Runtime:** The CellCog workspace. The [product page](https://cellcog.ai/) says an employee wakes on a schedule or when you ask, works the shift, and ends with a report. The next shift continues from memory of the last one. Each employee has an email inbox, a phone number, a task board, and a computer.

**Acts on:** The tools you already use, within the permission level you set. The page says that includes your browser and more than 1,500 tools, and that a purchase happens only after you approve it. Every task and email they touch is visible on one board.

**Extensions:** Other CellCog employees on the same account, who can be pulled into a job, and the Super Agent the page describes as the reasoning engine behind a shift. The page also says outside agents can start chats through the CellCog API. One credit balance pays for shifts, Super Agent chats, and those calls.

**Notes:** Hiring is free and the work spends credits. The FAQ says a new account starts with 200 credits and no card. Named roles on the page, such as a marketing manager or an executive assistant, are employees you hire inside this one product, not separate entries. The page says risky actions wait for approval and that they buy only what you approve.
