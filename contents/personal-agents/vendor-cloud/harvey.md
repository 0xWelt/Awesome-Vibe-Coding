Harvey is AI software for law firms and in-house legal teams. Its Agents product takes a piece of legal work, runs it, and returns cited output for a lawyer to review.

**Runtime:** The Harvey platform. The [Agents page](https://www.harvey.ai/platform/agents) says you can hand off one task or several, and Harvey coordinates agents that work in parallel. Scheduled agents run reports, updates, and reviews on a schedule you choose. The [homepage](https://www.harvey.ai/) describes the same platform for firms and legal departments, alongside Spaces, Vault, and contract tools.

**Acts on:** Documents, messages, and the systems you connect. The Agents page says inputs can include documents, images, video, or audio, and outputs can come back as reports, presentations, or interactive pages. Examples on that page include a regulatory briefing, deposition prep, deal-point lists, and contract drafting from a precedent. Connectors give agents access to Microsoft, document-management systems, and other tools the team already uses.

**Extensions:** Agent Builder, for a custom agent that uses your playbooks and preferences, plus the connectors above. Memory applies how you work across sessions. You preview the plan, change the scope, and approve it before Harvey begins. Nudges bring you back in when a judgment is needed. The page says every claim has a citation and every step is logged.

**Notes:** This entry is Harvey, filed here as a vendor-cloud agent with a legal tag. It is not a separate category. The Agents page says the lawyer keeps the judgment: review the plan, then review the work before it goes out. The homepage lists enterprise controls such as SAML SSO, audit logs, and IP allow-listing.
