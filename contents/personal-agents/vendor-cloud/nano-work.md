Nano Work (纳米Work) is 360's AI office platform. You assign the work, and cloud tasks keep running after the software is closed.

**Runtime:** The [product page](https://work.n.cn/) describes a cloud scheduler for daily, weekly, monthly, and ongoing jobs, and says the next batch does not need the software to stay open. The [user agreement](https://work.n.cn/agreement/iprotocol.html) is with 天津智炬科技有限公司 and its affiliates, referred to there as 360, and describes an installable client as well as cloud automation. The [privacy policy](https://work.n.cn/agreement/aprivacy.html) describes a member cloud-computer mode and a cloud drive that syncs generated files across devices where you are signed in.

**Acts on:** Tasks you describe, files you provide, and sites or software the automation is allowed to operate. The privacy policy says file access uses a storage permission you can turn off, and that visiting a logged-in site is something you authorize. The product page says key actions need confirmation, files are protected from casual deletion, and operations are logged.

**Extensions:** Skills, including a marketplace the product page says is security-reviewed, plus custom experts built from a conversation about your work. The product page also lists work connectors and a shared work memory of meeting notes, earlier outputs, and project files.

**Notes:** This entry is Nano Work, the office platform at work.n.cn. The product page is the source for the live plan list; it shows cloud task capacity that differs by plan. An iPad app is published under Beijing Qihoo Technology Co Ltd.
