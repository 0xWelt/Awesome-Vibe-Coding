Fyxer is an assistant across email, calendar, and meetings. The same assistant is reachable in Slack and Microsoft Teams, and a Flow can run a recurring job without a new request.

**Runtime:** Sign in with Google or Microsoft. The [homepage](https://www.fyxer.com/) says Fyxer connects to Gmail and Outlook, and the footer identifies the company as Fyxer AI Limited. The [learning hub](https://docs.fyxer.com/using-fyxer/get-more-from-your-fyxer-assistant) says chat, the inbox, Slack, Teams, Flows, and Spaces are ways to reach one assistant with the same context.

**Acts on:** Inbox, calendar, and meetings. The [help article](https://support.fyxer.com/article/meet-fyxer-your-ai-email-and-meeting-assistant) says Fyxer organizes the inbox, writes draft replies for you to review and send, and joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet to take notes and suggest follow-ups. In Slack or Teams, an @Fyxer mention can search that context, draft, or move a meeting. A Flow sends its result to Slack, Teams, email, or the Dashboard.

**Extensions:** Flows, which the learning hub says can be scheduled or triggered by a meeting or a label. Spaces share the emails, meetings, and files for a deal or client with the people in that space. The [integrations page](https://docs.fyxer.com/using-fyxer/integrations) also lists HubSpot, and an MCP connection that exposes the same email and meeting context to other clients.

**Notes:** Drafts stay in the inbox until you send them. The homepage lists a 14-day trial with a work email. This entry is the Fyxer assistant, not a general chat product.
