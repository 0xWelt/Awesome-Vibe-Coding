LobsterAI (有道龙虾) is NetEase Youdao's open-source desktop agent. The [product page](https://lobsterai.youdao.com/) says it installs on your computer, connects to local files, a terminal, a browser, and projects, and handles documents, data analysis, app building, research, and scheduled tasks.

**Runtime:** macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows. The FAQ says sessions, settings, and memory stay on the computer for now, and describes cloud sync as a later step. A scheduled job runs on that machine. From WeChat, WeCom, DingTalk, or Feishu you can send the computer a task and receive the result.

**Acts on:** Local files and projects, pages it opens, and the terminal. Examples on the page include building a small app, turning a spreadsheet into a report, a morning AI briefing, a research deck, and checking an ads dashboard.

**Extensions:** Skills and expert kits for scenes such as product, marketing, and data. The FAQ says LobsterAI is built on OpenClaw and can keep taking skills, tools, and plugins from that ecosystem. Official plan models named on the page include DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, and MiniMax. You can also add an API key or an Ollama model.

**Notes:** The FAQ says the code is MIT licensed at [netease-youdao/LobsterAI](https://github.com/netease-youdao/LobsterAI). This entry is the desktop agent. OpenClaw stays in [Self-hosted](../self-hosted/openclaw.md). The client can be downloaded free. The pricing page is the source for the official point and subscription plans.
