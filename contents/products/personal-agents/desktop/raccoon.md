SenseTime Raccoon (商汤小浣熊) is SenseTime's work assistant. The [product page](https://www.sensetime.com/cn/products/raccoon) says it plans a task, reads documents, spreadsheets, and images, and keeps going through analysis, writing, and a deliverable.

**Runtime:** A web app, Windows and Mac downloads, and a mobile client. The page says those clients continue the same task. For a team it also lists cloud, private-deployment, and appliance options, and connections to local files, Feishu, DingTalk, and company knowledge.

**Acts on:** The files and data you give it. The page lists planning, analysis and charts, content you can edit again, and knowledge Q&A. Scenes include marketing reviews, finance, HR, commerce, sales, and teaching materials.

**Notes:** The page says the English name Raccoon comes from "Another Comprehensive CO-pilOt Navigator". A personal edition and an enterprise edition are both described. The enterprise edition adds collaboration, permissions, company knowledge, single sign-on, and private deployment. The page says user documents are not used for model training.
