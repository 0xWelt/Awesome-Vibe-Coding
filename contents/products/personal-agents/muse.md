Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. You give it a goal or an everyday task, and it keeps working on that for you, including after you close the app.

**Runtime:** The Muse app on mobile and desktop, the web at [muse.ai](https://muse.ai/), and WhatsApp, by message or voice. You and your agent share a Muse Secure VM: a persistent, isolated Linux computer in the cloud with a full browser, and you can step in on that computer. Meta's [news post](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) says the rollout is in the US on iOS, Android, and muse.ai, with AI glasses listed as coming later.

**Acts on:** Apps you connect and the web, from the Secure VM. The product description covers browsing, purchases, documents, reminders, and tracking goals. The news post says Muse checks with you before sending an email or making a purchase, and that you choose whether a connection such as email can send or only read.

**Extensions:** Connectors you choose (the Meta product pages name email, calendar, and Instagram among everyday apps), built-in skills the agent picks for a task, and the Secure VM browser. Those pages also say Muse can build a tool when a task needs one that is not already there. A Sentinel on the same machine approves what reaches the internet and asks you when it needs to. You can allow an action once, always allow it, or deny it, and Muse keeps an audit trail.

**Notes:** Muse is free up to a usage limit, with a paid subscription for more use. This entry is the Muse agent. The linked product URL is https://muse.ai/, which serves the product. AI glasses are described as not available yet.
