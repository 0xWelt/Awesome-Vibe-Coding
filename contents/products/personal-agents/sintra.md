Sintra is a set of role-based AI employees for day-to-day business work. You add the business once, connect the tools, and set the approval level they have to follow.

**Runtime:** Web and mobile, described on the [product page](https://sintra.ai/) as available 24/7. Setup is: share how the business works, connect the tools, define priorities and approval levels, then let the employees run. The page says each worker runs as a chatbot you can message, and that work such as scheduled social posts continues while you are away.

**Acts on:** The connected tools for that role. The page describes social scheduling, inbox and calendar summaries, comment replies, meeting prep, SEO pages, and sales copy as the kind of work those roles take. Brand documents, tone, and guidelines are added once and reused.

**Extensions:** Named roles inside the one product, including a social media manager, a virtual assistant, an email marketer, an SEO specialist, and a sales manager. The page says you can keep more than one business profile and share a workspace with a team. It also says the employees connect to email, calendars, social platforms, and CRMs.

**Notes:** This file is Sintra. The named roles are not separate entries. The FAQ says they support a human team and do not replace it. Use the product page for the current price; the page shows a monthly and a yearly offer.
