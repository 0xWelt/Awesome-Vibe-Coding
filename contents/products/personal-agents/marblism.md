Marblism is a team of AI employees that each own an ongoing job. They start when something happens in that job, and they share one record of the business.

**Runtime:** The Marblism workspace. The [AI employees page](https://www.marblism.com/ai-employees) says an email, a call, or a quiet lead can start the work without you opening the app. You and human teammates review drafts in one group chat. The page says every plan includes unlimited human teammates.

**Acts on:** The tools each job already lives in. The page names Gmail, Outlook, and any IMAP inbox, Google and Outlook calendars, Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams for meetings, and Instagram, Facebook, X, and LinkedIn for social posts. The receptionist role answers on your existing business number once you forward it. You approve or edit the work, and later runs keep those corrections.

**Extensions:** Seven roles on one plan: Eva for inbox and calendar, Rachel for the phone, Stan for leads, Sonny for social media, Penny for SEO articles, Walter for the website, and Linda for contracts. They hand work to each other from the shared business record.

**Notes:** This file is the Marblism product. The roles above are jobs inside it, not separate entries. The page lists the plan at $44 a month billed monthly, or $24 a month billed yearly, for all seven employees and 50 hours of AI work a month. It also says your prompts, email, calls, and files are not used to train models.
