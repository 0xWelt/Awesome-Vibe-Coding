Xiaoyi Work (小艺Work) is Huawei's work assistant for office tasks, code, and design. The [product page](https://xiaoyi.huawei.com/work) says you state a goal and it splits the task, picks tools, and shows progress for you to review.

**Runtime:** A Windows client, with a HarmonyOS version listed as available to reserve. The phone edition can connect to a computer, follow a task, and use that computer's files and tools. A task can run on a cloud PC or on the local PC, chosen for the software and files it needs. The remote section says the computer keeps running the task after you step away, and the phone receives progress and the result. One phone can connect to more than one computer.

**Acts on:** The office, code, and design work named on the page, such as a project-report deck, comments on a function, and a poster. Experts are matched to the task, including research, writing, and programming.

**Notes:** This entry is Xiaoyi Work. [CodeArts](../coding-agents/standalone-ides/codearts.md) is Huawei Cloud's coding-agent family, including its own desktop workbench.
