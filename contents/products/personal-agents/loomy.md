Loomy is the AI work partner at loomy.xunfei.cn. You pick a working directory, and it handles file work, spreadsheets, research, and writing from that folder.

**Runtime:** A desktop app, a web app, a WeChat mini program, and a phone app. The [product page](https://loomy.xunfei.cn/) says the desktop app is the place for local files and heavier tasks, the web app can start a cloud task without an install, the mini program is for progress and notices, and the phone app is for decisions after you leave the desk. Desktop requirements on that page are macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon, and Windows 10 or later (x64).

**Acts on:** Folders you authorize. The page says files stay on your computer, work happens in those folders, and the contents are not uploaded. It also describes summarizing mail, meetings, and chat into to-dos, and analyzing local data into charts.

**Extensions:** Skills, chosen with `/`, and connectors for tools the page names: Feishu, DingTalk, WeChat, and Xiaohongshu. A 搭子团 can split a larger project. The page lists switchable models, including iFlytek Spark, DeepSeek, Qwen, Doubao, Kimi, and MiniMax.

**Notes:** The composer on the product page shows a default permission control next to the working directory. This entry is Loomy, the named work partner, not a general chat app.
