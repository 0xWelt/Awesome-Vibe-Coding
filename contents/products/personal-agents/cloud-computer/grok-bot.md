Grok Bot is a personal assistant you message like a teammate. It takes work across apps and sites you already use, on a computer of its own, and comes back when something needs approval.

**Runtime:** A desktop app and an iOS app talk to one persistent cloud computer shared by your bots (desktop, files, browser, and logins). The [product page](https://x.ai/bot) lists macOS, Windows, and iOS. The computer keeps working when the laptop is closed.

**Acts on:** Apps, websites, and inboxes that computer is signed into, including tools with no API. The [launch post](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot) describes work such as sales follow-up, office operations, and invoices that arrive in Gmail. The [Grok Bot 101](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-101) guide describes walking a bot through a retail-site comparison that adds items to a cart.

**Extensions:** The same guide says Grok Bot supports the same MCP servers, plugins, and skills as Cursor, and shows connected accounts such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Slack. A workflow you demonstrate can be saved as a routine and run on a schedule, and a bot can also listen for events. Bots can message each other. A review agent can allow, block, or escalate a proposed action; allow and block rules are written in natural language.

**Notes:** This entry is the Grok Bot product, not the Grok chat assistant. The August 11, 2026 launch post describes a beta for SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, selected Cursor plans, and an enterprise waitlist. The product-page FAQ retrieved for this entry lists Grok Bot on Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams, and Enterprise, and says sensitive actions can go through Auto Review before they run. Use the product page for the live plan list. That page describes sign-in as Cursor SSO. Cursor and other coding harnesses are listed in [Coding Agents](../../coding-agents/).
