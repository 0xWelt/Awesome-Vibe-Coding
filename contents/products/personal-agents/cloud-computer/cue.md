Cue (Cue by Manus) is Butterfly Effect's personal-agent product. The [site](https://cue.im) says each personal agent has its own email address, phone number, wallet, and computer.

**Runtime:** The Cue app. The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cue-by-manus/id6808613358) says a Cue can communicate, use digital services, and complete a purchase or payment after you approve it. The same listing says automations can run on a schedule or when a supported event happens. The cue.im homepage is a script shell; the sentence above is that page's meta description, and the behavior here is from the App Store listing, which names the same product.

**Acts on:** Everyday tasks in that listing, such as planning a trip, shopping, ordering where a business shows a Cue QR code, and using apps you connect, including email, calendar, and messaging. You confirm payment. Memory and Dream are how a Cue keeps preferences and plans between visits.

**Extensions:** Apps you authorize, and automations you define. The listing says you choose which apps Cue can use and what those permissions allow.

**Notes:** This entry is Cue. [Manus](manus.md) is the separate general agent from the same developer, BUTTERFLY EFFECT PTE. LTD. The App Store listing says Cue currently requires an invitation code, that creating an account is free, and that Plus membership is included at no charge once you have access.
