Dots are OpenAI's always-on personal agents. A dot keeps working toward your goals between conversations, on a computer of its own, and checks with you before actions that need approval.

**Runtime:** You create and message a dot in the ChatGPT app on iOS or Android, in the desktop app (including Windows), or on desktop web. Mobile web is not supported. The [intro post](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/) also describes messaging a dot in Slack and Teams, with texting still to come. Each dot has its own cloud computer and browser, which you can open and inspect. Connecting your own computer is optional, starts off, and is turned on from the ChatGPT desktop app on that machine.

**Acts on:** Apps you connect, sites in the dot's browser, and, if you allow it, files and the local browser on a computer you connect. While you are away, background "proactive research" uses connected apps in a read-only mode: it does not send messages, change app content, or control the browser or computer. Product pages describe work such as reviewing a calendar, preparing something for your approval, and carrying a project across tasks.

**Extensions:** Plugins. The intro post says a dot can connect to over 4,000 apps, and it starts from ChatGPT memory. With local computer access it can use local skills and, when its cloud browser is blocked, your local browser. Custom Rules let you allow an action, require approval, or block it. Actions that could affect accounts or share information go through auto-review, and some still need your approval. Changing a password stays with you.

**Notes:** The [getting-started article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot) says dots are rolling out to Pro users 18 and older outside the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the UK; to eligible Business Premium users; and to eligible Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces. That article describes an admin preview from November 4 through November 18, 2026, then default availability unless an admin opts out. This entry is Dots, not the ChatGPT assistant.
