WorkBuddy is Tencent's AI workbench for office work. You describe a task in ordinary language, and it plans the steps, uses tools, and returns a file you can review.

**Runtime:** Desktop apps for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows, plus a browser app, as listed on the [product page](https://www.workbuddy.ai/). The [Tencent Cloud product page](https://cloud.tencent.com/product/workbuddy) says long-running tasks keep going in the cloud after you close the client. The [quickstart](https://www.workbuddy.ai/docs/zh/workbuddy/Quickstart) says a new task opens in 日常办公 (everyday office) mode, and you can switch to a code-development mode.

**Acts on:** Folders you authorize. The quickstart describes reading those folders for batch work, and delivering documents, spreadsheets, and decks. The Tencent Cloud page also describes cloud-side spreadsheet processing you download when it finishes, and experts for jobs such as recruiting, research, and marketing.

**Extensions:** Skills, invoked with `/` in the quickstart, plus experts and connectors. The Tencent Cloud page says a project space can keep experts, skills, and connectors for a team to reuse, and that several agents can work on parts of one goal.

**Notes:** This entry is WorkBuddy, the office workbench. Tencent Cloud's [enterprise overview](https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/1831/134329) lists CodeBuddy as the separate coding product in the same family. CodeBuddy stays in [Coding Agents](../../coding-agents/). The quickstart also lets you pick a workspace and a default permission setting before a task runs.
