Claude Duet is Anthropic's proactive agent, and a separate app from Claude. You hand it work by message. It keeps going while you are away and waits for your ok when a step is sensitive.

**Runtime:** A standalone app for iOS and Android. On a computer, the [product page](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet) says you open it on the web at claude.ai. It works on its own computer in the cloud. You message it by text or voice, and the history stays in one thread.

**Acts on:** Apps you connect, and your inbox and calendar for a morning brief and drafts you approve. The product page describes flagging what needs you, drafting replies, finding unpaid invoices and drafting reminders, preparing payroll for review, getting contracts ready to send, and returning a doc, sheet, deck, or dashboard.

**Extensions:** The product page says Duet works with more than 6,000 connectors and plugins, and that it uses the memory, skills, and connectors on your Claude account. It asks before sensitive operations such as sending a message or paying. Approvals, logins, and safety checks run on that cloud computer.

**Notes:** Research preview, on a waitlist, for Claude Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers in the US. Team and Enterprise plans are not included yet. During the preview, Duet shares your Claude plan's usage limits, and background checks count toward them. The Claude chat app is not an entry here. Claude Code stays in [Coding Agents](../coding-agents/).
