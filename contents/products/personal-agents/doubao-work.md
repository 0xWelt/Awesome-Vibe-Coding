Doubao Work (豆包工作) is a named work product from Doubao. You hand it a job, and it carries the steps through to a file, a research write-up, or a scheduled run.

**Runtime:** A desktop client, the web, an entry inside the Doubao app, and an entry inside Feishu. The [help article on where to open it](https://www.doubao.com/work/docs/zh-cn/articles/462191106451-access) says the desktop client is the fuller task runtime, that Feishu needs client 7.75 or later, and that a phone uses the Doubao or Feishu entry because there is no separate mobile client. The [product page](https://www.doubao.com/work) says you can dispatch a task from your phone while you are away from the computer.

**Acts on:** Local files, the browser, and office deliverables. The product page says it can read, create, and modify local files, open web pages, fill information, and work across pages. It also lists PPT, documents, spreadsheets, research reports, and scheduled analysis or summary jobs. The product comparison says longer tasks, local files, and operating the local computer belong with Doubao Work.

**Extensions:** Skills for professional workflows, named on the product page, including office, research, and scheduled delivery. Feishu use follows the signed-in enterprise identity and the permissions that account already has.

**Notes:** This entry is Doubao Work, not Doubao chat. The [product comparison](https://www.doubao.com/work/docs/zh-cn/articles/562542181047-which-product) says Doubao is the general assistant for questions, search, and creation, while Feishu is the collaboration host. The [FAQ](https://www.doubao.com/work/docs/zh-cn/articles/805094220666-faqs) says the Feishu entry named 豆包 is this same product, and that 飞书 Aily, later called 豆包工作伙伴, is now a capability inside Doubao Work rather than a second product.
