Groceries, snacks, drinks, fresh food and household essentials delivered in as fast as 15 minutes, with many Gopuff locations open 24/7. Ask Grok for a weekly restock, tonight's dinner, a party run, a late-night craving or your usual: it shops your nearest Gopuff at app prices with no markups or service fees, builds your cart, shows you the total, places the order when you say yes and tracks it to your door.
