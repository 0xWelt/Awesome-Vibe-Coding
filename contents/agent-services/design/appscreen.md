Free browser editor for App Store and Google Play screenshots, with iPhone, iPad and Android device frames and store-ready PNG export. Its hosted MCP server at `https://appscreen.pro/mcp` (OAuth, needs an AppScreen Pro account) lets Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other MCP clients lay out slides, drop app screenshots into the frames, style backgrounds and export the final images. Setup guide: https://appscreen.pro/mcp
