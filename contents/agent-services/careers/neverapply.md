NeverApply matches open jobs to a person's resume, tailors that resume to a role, and fills employer application forms, including screening questions, from the resume and saved facts. Ask mode submits only after the person approves a job; Send mode submits from matches they have already bounded, and interview prep adds likely questions and talking points drawn from the resume. NeverApply publishes an MCP server for those same workflows.
