Curated slideshows and carousels for your brand on autopilot. Wavegrain reads a brand from its website, writes TikTok slideshows and Instagram carousels in the brand's voice from photos the user picks, then schedules and posts them. Its hosted MCP server at `https://wavegrain.com/api/mcp` (OAuth, registry name `com.wavegrain/mcp`) and the agent skill in `wavegrain/wavegrain-mcp` let Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other MCP clients make, schedule and track posts. Setup guide: https://wavegrain.com/mcp
