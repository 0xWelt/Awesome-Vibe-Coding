Free comparison guide that picks one AI coding tool per use case, with a public scoring rubric and dated price checks. Individual-plan prices below were checked against each vendor's own page on Oct 27, 2026 (USD per month, before tax). [Cursor](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing): Free $0 (limited usage), Plus $8, Premium $30 (4x Plus usage), Super $100 (15x), Ultra $200 (50x); on Premium and above, third-party models are billed separately at API list price. [Claude Code](https://claude.com/pricing) is included in Claude Pro ($20 monthly, or $17/month billed annually) and Max (from $100; 5x or 20x Pro usage) but not in Free; limits reset on a five-hour window with weekly caps on paid plans. [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans): Free $0 (2,000 completions a month), Pro $10 (includes $15 of AI credits), Pro+ $39 ($70 of credits), Max $100 ($200 of credits); completions don't use credits, and 1 credit = $0.01. Vendors change plans often, so check the linked pages before buying.
