name: "Shipped & Paid"
link: "https://shippedandpaid.com"
---

Directory of apps built mostly with AI coding tools (Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable, Bolt and others) that report revenue. Each case links its source and carries a proof label: Verified (read from Stripe or RevenueCat through a public profile such as TrustMRR), Screenshot, or Self-reported. Includes a free revenue calculator built from the same data.
