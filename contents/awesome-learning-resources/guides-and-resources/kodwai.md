Platform where developers solve real coding challenges on their own machine with their own AI coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex) and get scored on how well they direct the agent, across three axes: Direction, Outcome, and Lift. Built for practicing agentic coding and vibe coding, with a public leaderboard. Free for developers.
